# Dishari Fashion Show — Formation Rehearsal Hub

An interactive overhead-stage guide for rehearsing the Dishari Fashion Show formations across Acts 1, 2, and 3.

## Open the rehearsal hub

**[Launch the Formation Rehearsal Hub](https://joyeta88.github.io/dishari-fashion-show-rehearsal/)**

The hub works on phones, tablets, and computers.

## Master script

**[Read the complete fashion-show script](SCRIPT.md)**

`SCRIPT.md` is the controlled master version. A Google Docs copy may be used for collaboration and comments, with approved revisions transferred back here.

## How to use it

1. Select an act and scene from the menu.
2. Press **Replay Scene** to watch the formation.
3. Use **Pause** to stop and study positions.
4. Follow your assigned model label, such as A1, B2, or C3.
5. Refer to the master script for the actual scene timing, poses, props, and storytelling.

## Important note

The animations are accelerated 12-second previews designed to explain movement patterns. They do not represent the real performance speed.

## Stage orientation

- **Top:** Upstage / screen
- **Bottom:** Audience
- The diagrams use the full auditorium stage.
